Boards of sticky notes with no edges. Double-tap to add a note, drag it where it belongs, pinch to make it bigger or smaller. When you're done with one, drag it to the bin or pinch it until it crumples. Every delete offers Undo.

• Keep a separate board for each project, trip or idea
• Tap a note to write on it, and its menu changes the color
• Search finds a note on any board and opens the board right on it
• A short tour lets you try each gesture on a practice board
• Every gesture also has a button or menu item, and VoiceOver actions
• iPhone and iPad

Make Notes
On devices with Apple Intelligence, write a list and get a note per item, or write a topic and get ideas to move around. It runs on your device, so what you write stays there.

Sticky Notes Pro
One purchase, no subscription. The free app has three boards, ten Make Notes a month and four colors. Pro removes the limits and adds four more colors.
